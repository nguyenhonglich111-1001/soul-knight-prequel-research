Why the build works

The logic
  1. 1One damage type, all in. Cloudpiercer Shot and Scattershot deal Piercing damage. Piercing is one of the game's three Physical types, next to Slashing and Bludgeoning. So BladeheartBladeheartIncreases physical damage dealt by ?%, stacking up to ? times and lasting for ?s., Swordmaster's Ring, the Halfboots' slashes and Clockgear of the Archer's bullets all boost the main skill. Nobody puts a single point into an element.
  2. 2A self-feeding engine. Kiting is what a Ranger does anyway. Evanescent Halfboots turn that movement into BladeheartBladeheartIncreases physical damage dealt by ?%, stacking up to ? times and lasting for ?s. stacks, and Swordmaster's Ring turns a full stack into a burst. The build needs no extra button presses to keep its biggest buff running.
  3. 3Many different damage lines. The items word their bonuses differently: Skill DMG (Ensorcelled Wardrobe, Heart Pendant, Pandora's Gossamer Silks), bonus damage (Darksteel Helm, Harrier's Headguard, the cores, Fabled Savior's Boots), total damage (Inner Eye Necklace), and the enemy takes more damage (VulnerableVulnerableIncreases the total damage taken by enemies by ?% for ?s. from a charged Cloudpiercer Shot, the Clockgears, Chthonic Descent). If the game multiplies separate lines, as most ARPGs do, spreading across them beats stacking one. The numbers are encrypted, so this cannot be proven from the data.
  4. 4Defence traded for damage. Ensorcelled Wardrobe lowers every resistance. Pandora's Gossamer Silks curses you so you take more damage. Harrier's Headguard stops all shields. Vitality Gear spends your life. At range, those costs rarely come due.
  5. 5Talent skills do double duty. Talent skills are the chip skills such as Recuperation and Nebula Barrier. Each gear slot hangs a big effect on one: Vitality Gear stacks Attack Power, the Clockgears add a debuffing turret or orb, and Octagram Matrix turns the barrier into an aura that follows you.

If you are building toward this: Firmament's Caprice first, then the Halfboots + Swordmaster's Ring pair, since they only work together. Then Ensorcelled Wardrobe. Helm, necklace and cores are where the top players differ, so any of the ones shown is fine.
