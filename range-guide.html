The short version

  • Level on whatever dungeon is ~10 levels above you. Pick Ranger at level 12.
  • Scattershot carries you to 60. At 60, switch to the Cloudpiercer Shot build, with Rain of Arrows: Leonar as its support skill.
  • Grab any Legendary gear and any projectile Fatebound while levelling — do not overthink it.
  • Floor 400 is the checkpoint for the mid-game build. Floor 600 unlocks Aeonic.
  • Save Forge tickets, coins and scrolls. You will want them for the end-game build.

Kuma writes ranked lists with <, and it means better than — Heart Pendant < Fettered Fury < Censurer's Necklace means take the Heart Pendant if you can get it, and the Censurer's Necklace is the fallback. Lists below are rewritten best-first so there is nothing to decode.

Stage 1 — Levelling

Level 1 → 60

All of this is much easier with a title that boosts your attack by 50 or 100. If you have one, skip ahead to Halcyon Cemetery.

  1. 1No title yet? Farm mobs in Brinewash Grotto and kill the boss there.
  2. 2Got a title? Start straight on Halcyon Cemetery instead.
  3. 3Then head to Arid Sands and grind to level 12, which is where you pick your class. Pick Ranger.
  4. 4From there, just beat whatever dungeon is about 10 levels above you.
  5. 5At level 30, start running the level 45 dungeons. There are two — either is fine.
  6. 6At level 50, run any Pandora's Labyrinth that offers projectile Fatebounds — and the ones offering Onslaught too.

Use Scattershot as your main skill. Once you unlock its specialization, take any damage-increase spec. Once the rest of the skills open up, use whatever makes sense together. Too lazy to read the specs? Copy a leaderboard spec layout and move on.

Your levelling skill

Scattershot

You use your ranged weapon to fire a cluster of arrows that deal ?% Piercing damage to an enemy, and upon doing so, split into ? fragments that each deal ?% bonus damage (physical).

The whole early game. Fires a cluster that fragments on hit — free clear speed while you have no gear.

For gear, use any Legendary you find and any projectile-related Fatestone. That is genuinely all the thought it deserves at this stage. It gets much easier once you actually understand what each piece does.

Good weapons for the class start dropping around here. If you find an Insane-rarity weapon and you are holding a Prime one, use the Insane weapon anyway — rarity wins.

Stage 2 — Early mid-game

Level 60 → floor 400

At level 60 the real problems start. Switch to the Cloudpiercer Shot build — usually called the torrential build — for much better damage. Cloudpiercer Shot is the main skill; Rain of Arrows: Leonar backs it up. You can copy a leaderboard build wholesale, with three exceptions you should pick yourself: equipment, Fatebounds and Axial Incarnate.

Support skill. A creeping rain of arrows that walks toward your target — parks extra damage on the whole room while Cloudpiercer Shot does the heavy lifting.

Onslaught

Whenever you crit, you fire additional projectile fragments, and the number of projectiles launched by your next skill attack is increased.

Crits add fragments and load up your next skill cast. This is the multiplier that makes the build work.

Fragments burst out of anything you kill or Exsanguinate.

Death from Afar

Damage is increased when no enemies are close.

Free damage for playing at range, which is where you want to be anyway.

Then just keep running Pandora's Labyrinth and Enigmine for gear and resources. Save some of it — Forge tickets, coins and scrolls — for the end-game build. Suffer.

Do all of this right and you should end up around floor 400, maybe further.

Why the Cloudpiercer Shot build?

1. It does not need high spec levels or tight restrictions to put out big damage.
2. It combines Cloudpiercer Shot and Rain of Arrows, whose specs stack into higher damage.
3. It is not as laggy as the Razorward or Scattershot builds.
4. It is easy to play.
5. Bows are cool.

Stage 3 — Aeonic

Floor 600+

At floor 600 you can start running Aeonic. Run a lot of it, climbing the difficulties: Insane, then Ultra Insane, then Insanely Insane. Yes, the naming is ridiculous.

Once you have the Underworld summon core you can start chasing Calamity Fatestones. Good luck getting a natural 4-socket Fatebound drop — Chillyroom made that thing a myth. Better odds on a lottery ticket.

If you are already doing 30T–50T damage, start farming better gear. Copy the leaderboard for items at this point; the marginal calls stop mattering.
